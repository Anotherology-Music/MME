CC Macros
A macro is an editing aid: attaching one to a note expands it into ordinary CC points at that note's position. Macro notes are drawn amber in the piano roll.
Pitch bindings
on
Bind a macro to a pitch and every note at that pitch carries it automatically — put the macro on C1 and then just write a note wherever the kick lands. Assigning a macro to a single note with "→ Note" above overrides its binding, as the one-off escape hatch.

Tempo Map
Beat-map to audio: Alt+click a beat line to pin a tempo point there (BPM unchanged), then Alt+drag a later beat onto the waveform where it belongs — only the stretch between the two is re-tempoed. Audio is never moved or time-stretched. Click a marker to edit or remove it.
